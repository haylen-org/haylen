# Contributing to Haylen

Haylen is free for everyone, for personal and commercial work, forever. The engine, its tools, its templates, its samples and all of their art, sounds and documentation are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE), and no part of Haylen will ever ask for a fee, a royalty, a revenue share or a paid tier.

## Your contribution becomes part of Haylen

Everything you contribute, whether code, documentation, images, sprites, tilesets, icons, sounds, music, fonts or any other content, becomes part of Haylen under the same MIT License as the rest of the repository. By opening a pull request or sending a contribution in any other way, you agree that:

- Anyone may use, copy, change, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell your contribution, in personal and commercial projects, with or without Haylen, under the terms of the [MIT License](LICENSE).
- Your contribution is your own work, or work you have the right to give under these terms, and it carries no condition that conflicts with them.
- You do not withdraw or restrict this permission later, so every app made with Haylen can rely on it.

## What a contribution may contain

The art of the repository is made for the project. A contribution never includes images made by others, the names, art or exact look of existing games, apps, companies, studios or artists, or code copied from projects under incompatible licenses. Sounds are made for the project or released into the public domain (CC0), and fonts come with an open license whose license file travels with them.

## How to contribute

The file [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the rules every change follows: the code organization and naming, the formatting, the writing standard, the tests and the documentation that come with every change, and the format of commit messages. A change is complete when it is implemented, exported to Lua, tested and documented, and when `haylen test`, `haylen format --check` and the tests of the tools pass.
